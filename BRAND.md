# Exfil brand notes for creators

Exfil is a trading app where every holder of a coin is a rat on a hill. The
higher your rat stands, the more it's up. You drop in, you climb, and you
try to get out with the bag before the hill turns. Get out with the bag, or
get rekt.

## The rats

**The Scout** is the hero: grey-white rat, pink ears and nose, olive helmet
with orange goggles, olive vest, a big black duffel that reads EXFIL. In the
app it's every player's own rat. (`rats/full-body/cute.png`)

**The Crew** are the outfits players can wear: Ronin, Hacker, Hacker ·
Shades, Medic, Captain, Straw Hat, Fixer, Hooded Ranger, Fresh Cut, Miner,
Biker, Skater, Unicyclist. **Pixel** is the original pixel-art rat.
**Operators** (Field Rat, Night Ops, Desert Raider) are the realistic
tactical rats.

**Looks and signs** mean things in the app — use them that way and the
jokes land with players:

| Look | Meaning in the app |
| --- | --- |
| Pinkie (hairless pink rat) | a wallet first funded under a day ago |
| Sleeper (eyes shut, lamps off) | "Lost Seed Phrase": held 30+ days, no trades anywhere |
| Rekt (dead rat, belly up) | sold at a heavy loss |
| Golden parachute | sold everything at a profit |
| Gold crown | a top trader on that coin |
| Dev rat (eye patch, money sack) | the coin's creator |
| Payday (duffel filling with coins) | a coin that pays its holders |
| Small / round / broad build | under 0.1% / 0.1–1% / over 1% of supply |

## Words

- It's **Exfil** in sentences, **EXFIL** on the bag and the token.
- Verbs: drop in, climb, extract, get out with the bag, get rekt.
- Write tickers without a dollar sign: "EXFIL", not "$EXFIL".
- Tone: short, dry, a little gallows humour. The rats are scrappy, not
  cruel.
- Never promise gains. Don't call anything financial advice.

## Colours

The app's dark night palette, if you want your content to sit next to it:

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stage | `#050706` | background |
| Panel | `#102023` | cards |
| Ink | `#F5F3F0` | text |
| Muted | `#A3ADA6` | secondary text |
| Pill green | `#B9F27C` | buy, up, profit |
| Red | `#F2726E` | sell, down, loss |
| Gold | `#E9C463` | crowns, wins |
| Amber | `#F2B54E` | warnings, goggles glow |

Fur and kit tint colours are in `rats/layers/manifest.json`.

## Type

The app uses Inter, free under the SIL Open Font License from

display face; any blocky pixel font matches.
